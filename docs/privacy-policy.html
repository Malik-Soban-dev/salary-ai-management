Salary AI — Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 13, 2026 · Contact: YOUR_SUPPORT_EMAIL (replace before submitting to the stores)

Salary AI helps you turn your salary into a spending plan. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the control you keep. The short version: we store only what the app needs to plan your money, we never sell it, and you can export or delete everything at any time.

1. What we collect

  • Account: email address and a salted password hash. We never see your plain password.
  • Financial profile you enter: expected income, recurring commitments (rent, utilities…), savings goals, currency, language, country and timezone.
  • Spending records you choose to add: amount, merchant description, category and date. Salary AI does not connect to your bank and never asks for bank credentials, card numbers, or account logins.
  • Assistant conversations: messages you send the in-app assistant, so it can answer with your plan context.
  • Technical logs: minimal server logs (request time, status) for security and reliability.

2. What we never do

  • We never sell or rent your personal data.
  • We never run ads or trackers inside the app.
  • We never move money. Salary AI only plans; it cannot touch any account.
  • We never use your financial data to train public AI models.

3. AI features

The in-app assistant answers questions about your own plan. When the upgraded AI mode is enabled, your message plus the minimum plan context needed to answer (e.g. your safe-to-spend figure) is sent to our AI provider under a data-processing agreement, without your name or email attached. You can use the full app with the AI assistant disabled — it is optional.

4. Storage & security

  • Data is stored on servers secured with transport encryption (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Passwords are stored as salted hashes; sessions are token-based and expire.
  • Every record is keyed to your user id — tenant isolation is enforced server-side.

5. Your rights and controls

  • Export: a machine-readable copy of everything (profile, plan, transactions) is available in-app (GET /v1/profile/export) or on request.
  • Deletion: request account deletion from the app or by email; we delete your records within 30 days and confirm.
  • Access & correction: edit any field of your profile and records directly in the app at any time.

6. Children

Salary AI is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your region) and we do not knowingly collect their data.

7. Changes

If this policy changes materially we will notify you in the app before the change takes effect. The data rights in section 5 always remain.

Data safety summary for store review: data collected: email, user-entered financial information, in-app messages. Purpose: app functionality only.
